**Article Text:**  
A team at MIT has unveiled an AI model capable of predicting heart disease risk using data from standard blood tests and patient histories. Unlike traditional methods relying on imaging or invasive tests, this AI algorithm leverages machine learning techniques to analyze routine data, providing a cost-effective and accessible diagnostic tool.
